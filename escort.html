Service Scope Non‑clinical

KIWI Serenity Limited provides non‑clinical coordination and support services only, including:

  • Appointment coordination and scheduling assistance
  • Bilingual escort support for general communication
  • Hospital/clinic navigation and guidance
  • Registration and payment process assistance
  • Receipts and document organisation
  • Transportation coordination (where purchased)

We are not a medical provider. We do not provide medical diagnosis, medical advice, treatment recommendations, or clinical decision‑making.

Medical Communication Disclaimer Important
  • We are not medical professionals.
  • We do not provide medical interpretation for diagnosis or treatment decisions, medical diagnosis, or medical advice.
  • Any diagnosis, treatment discussion, consent, and clinical decision‑making must be conducted directly between the patient and the licensed doctor/clinic.
  • Our bilingual escort may assist with general communication and non‑clinical paperwork, but does not replace a certified medical interpreter where legally or clinically required.
Payment Terms Deposit

To secure your booking, a USD $200 deposit is required. The deposit will be deducted from your final package price.

  • Deposit: USD $200 — non‑refundable once appointment scheduling has been confirmed.
  • Remaining balance: due 14 business days before the first scheduled appointment date.
  • Payments are required within 10 business days of the invoice date unless otherwise specified.

Late bookings / urgent scheduling: If the first appointment is within 14 business days, the remaining balance is due immediately upon confirmation (or by the due date stated on the invoice).

Hospital / Clinic Fees Pass‑through

Medical/dental fees are determined and charged by the provider (clinic/hospital). We may assist with coordination, but you pay provider fees directly to the provider unless otherwise agreed in writing.

If a hospital/clinic registration fee is required:

  • It is determined and charged according to the provider's official fee schedule.
  • This fee is non‑refundable once the appointment has been confirmed by the provider.
  • If the actual registration fee is lower than the amount collected, the difference will be refunded.
  • Examinations, imaging, laboratory tests, procedures, medications, materials, and any other provider charges are not included and are charged separately by the provider.
Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Cancellation

  • > 14 days before appointment: 70% refund
  • 7–14 days before appointment: 40% refund
  • < 7 days before appointment: no refund

Any non‑refundable third‑party fees already incurred will be deducted from refundable amounts. Receipts provided when available.

Rescheduling

  • One reschedule free of charge if requested > 14 days before appointment, subject to provider availability.
  • Rescheduling within 14 days may incur additional coordination fees and/or provider charges.
  • If a provider declines a reschedule, the booking may be treated as a cancellation under the policy above.
